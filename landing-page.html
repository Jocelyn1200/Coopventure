The Coop Venture

A smarter path from school to career

The Coop Venture is building an AI-powered career platform that helps students approach internships and job applications more strategically. By designing around real student challenges and feedback, we're making the application process clearer, faster, and more confidence-building from start to finish.

Explore insights

What we are focusing on first

1) Better job matching

  • Match students to roles based on skills and goals.
  • Improve role relevance and filter quality.
  • Reduce wasted time on poor-fit applications.

2) Resume + application support

  • Actionable resume feedback before applying.
  • Guidance aligned to real job requirements.
  • Simpler process for repetitive applications.

3) Career readiness training

  • Technical training (modeling, Excel, role skills).
  • AI mock interviews with performance feedback.
  • Preparation built around real job descriptions.

Who this is for

  • Primary: Undergraduate students in NYC, including international students.
  • Partners: Universities looking to improve placement outcomes.
  • Employers: Companies that want better-prepared entry-level candidates.

About us

The Coop Venture is an early-stage AI-powered career platform focused on helping students apply to internships and jobs more strategically. We are designing the product around real student feedback to reduce confusion, improve confidence, and make the process faster.
